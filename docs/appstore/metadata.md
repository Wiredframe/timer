## Description (4000)
Plain Tabata Timer is a free interval timer that does one thing and does it well. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription, no account.

Set the number of sets, how long you work and how long you rest. Press start. That's it.

WHAT YOU GET
• Sets, work and rest on one screen, adjustable in 5-second steps. Your settings are remembered.
• 10 seconds to get ready, which you can skip.
• A fill that rises with the time: lime while you work, aqua while you rest. You can read it from across the room.
• Clear sounds for 3, 2, 1, the start of work, the start of rest and the end. Your music or podcast keeps playing.
• Pause, skip back, skip forward or stop at any time. The total time left is always shown.
• Keeps exact time with the screen locked. The screen stays on during a workout.
• Light and dark mode, following your iPhone.

WHAT YOU DON'T GET
• No workout library, no history, no streaks, no social feed.
• No ads, no tracking, no data leaves your iPhone.
• No account, no subscription, no in-app purchases.

Works for Tabata, HIIT, EMOM-style intervals, stretching, planks or anything else that needs work and rest on a clock.
